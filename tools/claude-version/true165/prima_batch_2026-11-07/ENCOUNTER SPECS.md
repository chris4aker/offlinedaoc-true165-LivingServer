Research only: what period sources say each encounter did, what the server already has, and what needs new code.

## Vale of Balor creeper

### ixthiar creeper (PLACE, 1 spawn(s), level 45-45)

Position: Landmark only. No CapnBry radar sightings, no Uthgard dots and no Prima/Atlas map label (I checked the Vale of Balor map page layout, SI Prima p.205). Prima SI p.211 says the creeper 'immediately leaves its brood the moment it spawns'. In our world the brood is the ixthiar nest: Ixthiar Broodmother at local (22268,18223), plus the dense nest of 12 faction-87 ixthiar decimaters at local X 17-21k, Y 17.5-22k, ringed by reapers and doomcallers. That matches the Warcry Fylara's Armor spoiler, whose nest is 'across the wooden bridge and to the northwest' on the road SW from Aalid Feie; Fylara is at local (48258,21954). Home spawn is on the east side of that nest, between the decimaters and the Broodmother: local (21000,19000) = world (283144,346680). Z: K.ground/groundz165 gives no floor anywhere in zone 183 (it even fails at existing mob spots), so Z 3725 is the mean of the 4 nearest live rows (3710, 3712, 3727, 3760; all within 64). A post-period Allakhazam comment (2007-03-02) puts a creeper at loc 23639,11908, about 7k north of the brood inside the reaper/doomcaller field. That backs up 'wanders out into the vale' but was not used as the spawn point.

PERIOD EVIDENCE
(1) Prima Shrouded Isles (2002), Vale of Balor, 'Enhanced Monster AI' box (txt line 29691; layout line 9414): 'A creeper immediately leaves its brood the moment it spawns. It then ventures out into the vale and starts spawning more ixthiar. If there are no players to control the ixthiar, they will spread over 1/3 of the entire area.' SI Prima monster table: level 45 (the layout text's '42-45' is a two-row column shift; si_tables.json and Allakhazam both give 45).
(2) Allakhazam/Illia bestiary cmob=5511, Wayback capture 2003-09-19 (entry last updated 2003-01-08): Insect, 'Creepers are known to leave it's brood and wander out into the open and spawn babies, and can possilby cover 1/3 the area', min/max level 45, Vale of Balor, lowers Ixthiar Swarm. No loot, no comments in period.
(3) Post-period only, corroboration not authority: the live ZAM page (updated 2007) adds 'when pulling one also brings a Ixthiar Decimater', a 2007-03-02 comment gives loc 23639,11908 'in the Ixthiar Woods', and it lists the loot.
(4) Prima SI zone text (p.208): ixthiar are 'social pack MOBs and have the ability to call for help when outnumbered'; bring crush weapons.
No period source gives a respawn timer, a spawn interval, an add count or the add type beyond 'more ixthiar'/'babies'. Those values below are marked CHOICE.

WHAT EXISTS IN THE SERVER: nothing for this encounter. No class or brain mentions ixthiar or creeper (I grepped GameServer scripts, namedmobs, ai/brain, quests and mode165). Patterns to reuse:
- scripts/mobs/Strangler.cs and BotonidSeedling.cs: a GameNPC subclass that calls SetOwnBrain(new XBrain()) in AddToWorld. The Mob row then carries ClassType 'DOL.GS.Scripts.<Class>' with Brain '' or 'DOL.AI.Brain.<Brain>'; the DB already does this for DOL.GS.Scripts.Strangler / DOL.AI.Brain.StranglerBrain.
- scripts/namedmobs/Galladoria/SpindlerBroodmother.cs SpawnSplinder() + class SBAdds: timer-spawned adds with RespawnInterval = -1, LoadedFromScript = true and their own faction, never saved to the DB.
- scripts/mobs/AlbionMobs/Eques.cs: Body.WalkTo(point, speed) for walking legs.
- ai/brain/StandardMob/BafFriends.cs: the existing faction 'call for help' covers Prima's social-pack note through faction 87. No new code is needed for that.
NEW CODE NEEDED: class DOL.GS.Scripts.IxthiarCreeper : GameNPC and brain DOL.AI.Brain.IxthiarCreeperBrain : StandardMobBrain. The spawn row uses ClassType 'DOL.GS.Scripts.IxthiarCreeper', Brain 'DOL.AI.Brain.IxthiarCreeperBrain' (or '' if the class sets its own brain), NPCTemplateID 60162558, FactionID 87, Level 45, RoamingRange 0 (the brain does the walking).

BEHAVIOUR SPEC
A. Spawn and respawn (period: Prima 2002 and Allakhazam 2003): the creeper appears at the home spot (the brood). On AddToWorld, without waiting for an idle tick, it picks a first destination 1500-3000 units from home, pointing away from the nest centre (local 19500,19700), and walks there at speed 191 (template MaxSpeed). This is 'immediately leaves its brood'.
B. Wandering (period: 'ventures out into the vale'/'wander out into the open'): while it has no aggro, each time it arrives (or every 20-40 s if stuck) it picks a new leg 1000-2500 units long, biased outward until it reaches the leash edge, then random. CHOICE, clamped to the SPREAD AREA: zone-183 local X 8,000-45,000, Y 5,000-37,000. That is the bounding box of all live ixthiar in the vale (X 10.4k-47.1k, Y 7.0k-35.1k) plus a margin, about 1.18e9 units², roughly 28% of the 65536² zone ≈ Prima's '1/3 of the entire area'. Exclude a 4000-unit radius around the Grove of Aalid Feie (local ~49600,25000) and do not path into the Fomorian town to the south (local Y > 37k). Region 181 has no usable navmesh in the helper, so use straight WalkTo legs and keep Z by the server's normal ground follow.
C. Seeding more ixthiar (period: 'starts spawning more ixthiar'/'spawn babies'). Every 90-180 s (CHOICE, random each time), while the creeper is alive, out of combat and inside the spread area, it places ONE offspring at its current position (±100 units).
  - Offspring type, CHOICE: 'ixthiar decimater' from NpcTemplate 60162559, level 39-41 (Prima SI decimater levels), model 686, faction 87, RoamingRange 200 like the family rows, RespawnInterval -1, LoadedFromScript = true, CanDropLoot as normal decimater loot (the existing 'ixthiar decimater' MobXLootTemplate). Why decimater: it is the lowest-level ixthiar in the core wood, it makes up the brood cluster the creeper spawns from, and the post-period ZAM note says a creeper pull 'also brings a decimater'. Option: a weighted pick from decimater/reaper/doomcaller templates 60162559/60162562/60162560.
  - Population cap, CHOICE for the bot-scale rule: at most 24 live offspring per creeper. At the cap it keeps wandering but stops seeding until offspring are killed. This is how players 'control the ixthiar'.
  - Offspring persist after the creeper dies; they are not saved, so a server restart clears them. Never spawn an offspring within 1500 of a player-realm guard or town NPC.
D. Combat: an ordinary level-45 insect with no special attacks or abilities in any period source. Faction-87 social assist comes from the existing BafFriends. No adds at a health threshold (none recorded in period). It stops seeding and wandering while it has aggro. After combat it does NOT return to the brood: it resumes wandering from where it is (that is how it spreads). The template's TetherRange 4500 should be ignored or kept relative to the current position; otherwise it would yank the creeper back to the nest.
E. Death and respawn: the creeper respawns at the home spot after RespawnInterval and repeats A-C. 900 s is a CHOICE; there is no period figure (the family rows use 80-300 s, which would flood the vale). Keep exactly ONE creeper row; Prima describes 'a creeper' leaving 'its brood'.
F. Performance (bot scale 1-2k bots per realm): ThinkInterval of at least 1500 ms. Track offspring in a List on the creeper, not with region scans. Run the seeding timer as an ECSGameTimer only while alive.
G. Loot: no period drop is tied to the creeper. The existing MobXLootTemplate 'Ixthiar Creeper' (id 5511) points to an empty LootTemplate; leave it empty. Ixthiar Healing / Nature's Aid / Rejuvenating Stone existed in period (Freddyshouse sale posts 2003-09-21 and 2003-11-19; ItemTemplate rows Ixthiar_*_Stone exist, dropped by nothing in our DB). The only creeper link is the post-period ZAM loot list (page updated 2007), so adding them is an owner decision, not a period fact. Coin: Coin165 Insect body default.
RELATED, out of scope: our Ixthiar Broodmother is a fixed 300 s spawn. Period/early-2004 sources (Warcry Fylara's Armor spoiler, CSR reply 2004-04-11) say she pops in one of SIX large stumps once the reapers and doomcallers around the nest are cleared. That is a separate encounter.

Notes: PLACE as one scripted spawn at the ixthiar brood. Copy NpcTemplate 60162558 (model 686 Praying Mantis (Red), size 55, Insect, faction 87 Ixthiar Swarm, speed 191, aggro 200/range 500 like every live ixthiar row). Level 45 is fixed: Prima SI table and the Allakhazam 2003-09-19 capture both give 45-45, and the template's 45;49 is not period. The spreading/seeding behaviour needs new code (IxthiarCreeper + IxthiarCreeperBrain); no existing class implements it. Timers, add type and cap are labelled CHOICE in the script because period sources give none. Until the script exists, a plain row would just stand at the nest. If the owner wants it live before the code, use RoamingRange ~1500 as a stop-gap and note it is not the period behaviour. Live family base for stats/brain if needed: ixthiar decimater Mob_ID 0411d4f8-b60... / ixthiar reaper rows at the nest. Navmesh ground for zone 183 is unavailable in groundz165, so Z comes from neighbouring live rows.

## Cothrom Gorge Cuinn seedlings

### garran treant seedling (PLACE, 6 spawn(s), level 60-60)

Position: Spawned by script, not a static Mob row. Period players report the seedlings come out of the town when Cuinn calls for help (Freddyshouse, Xanthian 2003-03-13 and 2003-03-17). Period players could not find any standing seedling: Allakhazam cmob=5898 comment of 2003-06-28 ('looked all over Cothrom Gorge... haven't found him') and item 8784 comment of 2003-06-13 ('where is Garran Treant Seedling? i can't find that'). CapnBry radar has no sighting and Uthgard has no dot. The 6 points below are offsets about 220 units around Cuinn's live spawn point (Mob 100027804 at 335048,315288,Z2164, the Outlander Town far SE, local 56.5k,53.1k in zone 184). Region 181 has no navmesh, so groundz returns None. Z is copied from Cuinn's row; nearby rows are 2164-2216: Treal 2188, garran treant 2216. The script should place adds at SpawnPoint.Z, as Anurigunda.cs does. Headings face Cuinn's spawn point.

Parent encounter: CUINN (Mob_ID 100027804, Cothrom Gorge, Outlander Town). The seedlings exist only as Cuinn's adds.

PERIOD EVIDENCE
(a) Freddyshouse, old.Xanthian, 2003-03-13 18:33 UTC ('help with sc template') and 2003-03-17 ('They Mysterys of HyBrasil'). Cuinn is about level 65-70 and takes a beating. At 10% health he calls for help, and the whole town comes along with 6 treant seedlings that con red to a level 50. The seedlings drop the jewels, about 4-5 jewels per pull. Groups pulled Cuinn to the crossroads far from town so they could finish him before the adds arrived. Clearing the town before the pull means fewer adds.
(b) Patch 1.61 notes, quoted on Freddyshouse 2003-04-02: the drop rate for the garran treant seedlings that are part of the Cuinn encounter in Hibernia was adjusted to the appropriate level. The pre-1.61 rate was a bug (a player said he made about 30 plat from it).
(c) Prima SI, Cothrom Gorge monster table: garran treant seedling 60. Allakhazam cmob=5898 (captures 2003-04-19, last updated 2003-01-08): Plant, level 60-60, Cothrom Gorge. Killing it lowers faction with Z'zozicck Zhxizzckrrk, The Watcher, Tree of Ages and Outland Residents.
(d) Allakhazam items 8782, 8783 and 8784 (captured 2003-08/09) say each is dropped by the Garran Treant Seedling in Cothrom Gorge.
(e) Players could not find a seedling in the world (zam comments 2003-06-13 and 2003-06-28). This shows the seedlings are not static spawns.

TRIGGER
Cuinn is in combat and his HealthPercent drops to 10 or below. This fires once per spawn or reset cycle. The period wording of the shout is not recorded. Use a neutral broadcast such as 'Cuinn calls for help!', the same style as VagdushBrain.

SEQUENCE ON TRIGGER
1. Town call-for-help (period: 'the whole town comes along'). Take every living NPC of faction 98 (Outland Residents) within about 4500 units of Cuinn's SpawnPoint, not his current position. He may have been pulled to the crossroads, and period groups relied on the town having to travel to them. Copy Cuinn's aggro list to each of them (AddAggroListTo, as in VagdushBrain and AnurigundaBrain). Townsfolk who are already dead do not come, which matches 'clear the town first'. My suggestion, not period data: leave out service NPCs (Guild set: merchants, healer, smith, enchanter).
2. Spawn exactly 6 garran treant seedlings at Cuinn's SpawnPoint plus the offsets above, which is the town, not Cuinn's current spot. Each seedling gets RespawnInterval -1 and is put into combat against Cuinn's aggro list, starting with his current target. The seedlings then walk or run from town to the fight.

ADDS
6 x 'garran treant seedling': level 60, body Plant (10), model 946, size 50, FactionID 98, speed 191. Melee stats come from the garran treant template 60161210 with Level forced to 60. No NpcTemplate 'garran treant seedling' exists yet, so add one (based on 60161210, Level 60, Size 50) or set the values in code. The seedlings keep fighting after Cuinn dies, because the period groups killed Cuinn first and then the seedlings for the jewels. Do NOT kill them on Cuinn's death. This differs from ElroTheAncient.Die, which kills its adds.

RESET / DESPAWN
When Cuinn leaves combat (no aggro or wiped, CheckProximityAggro false), remove the seedlings that are still alive and clear the trigger flag. This is the AnurigundaBrain RemoveAdds pattern. Seedlings that outlive Cuinn and have no targets should go back to their spawn spot and be removed. A 60-second idle timer is an implementation choice, not period data. They never respawn on their own. The next set comes with Cuinn's next spawn. Cuinn's live RespawnInterval is 80 (seconds, multiplied by 1000 in GameNPC.LoadFromDatabase), which is very short for a named target. Period players farmed him repeatedly, but no period respawn time was found. Owner's call.

LOOT
MobXLootTemplate row 'garran treant seedling' -> LootTemplate 'garran treant seedling' already exists with DropCount 2. The LootTemplate has NO rows, so nothing drops today. Add Petrified_Treant_Seed, Crystalized_Treant_Seed and Frozen_Treant_Seed (all three ItemTemplates exist). Period rate: before the fix, about 4-5 jewels from the 6 seedlings of one pull. After the 2003-04-02 fix the rate was lowered to an unstated 'appropriate level', and the seeds were still farmed and sold in numbers in Sept 2003 (Freddyshouse WTS 2003-09-15, 'several of each'). No exact post-fix percentage exists. Pick a modest per-seedling chance and lower DropCount to 1. That is an owner/loot decision. Item data mismatches against period Allakhazam pages: Crystalized period is ML50, level to attain 49, but the DB says 47. Petrified (8782) period: 5 charges of a 95 cold DD. Frozen (8784) period: 5 charges of a 95 cold DD, 2.6 s cast. All three DB rows have SpellID 0 and Charges 0.

ALREADY IN THE SERVER
There is no Cuinn script, brain or seedling code anywhere in GameServer: grep for cuinn/seedling/treant finds only ElroTheAncient, Hurionthex, TheLostSeed quest, BotonidSeedling and a bot bench. The live Cuinn is a plain DOL.GS.GameNPC with Brain '' and FactionID 0, although his NpcTemplate 60159526 has FactionID 98. Patterns to reuse:
- scripts/namedmobs/Fomor/Anurigunda.cs: AnurigundaBrain spawns waves of adds once at 40% and 20% HP (RespawnInterval -1), removes them on reset, and BAFs PackageID-tagged NPCs. Its AnurigundaAdds class and AnurigundaAddsBrain show how an add class is built.
- scripts/mobs/AlbionMobs/Vagdush.cs: VagdushBrain calls for help once at 10% or below and copies its aggro list to nearby NPCs.

NEW CODE NEEDED
1. DOL.AI.Brain.CuinnBrain : StandardMobBrain, implementing the trigger, steps 1-2 and the reset above.
2. Optionally a DOL.GS.GarranTreantSeedling : GameNPC add class with its own brain, or plain GameNPCs built from the new NpcTemplate.

HOW THE SPAWN ROW REFERENCES IT
GameNPC.LoadFromDatabase builds the brain from Mob.Brain with ScriptMgr.GameServerScripts. On Mob 100027804, set Brain = 'DOL.AI.Brain.CuinnBrain' and ClassType stays DOL.GS.GameNPC; a DOL.GS.Cuinn class is an alternative. Also set FactionID = 98 so the Outland faction link works. The seedlings themselves get NO Mob rows.

Notes: These are the 6 adds of the Cuinn encounter, so the spawns are script offsets around Cuinn's spawn point, not DB rows. Evidence: Freddyshouse 2003-03-13/17 (10% call for help, the town plus 6 seedlings, jewels from the seedlings) and patch 1.61 of 2003-04-02 ('garran treant seedlings that are part of the Cuinn encounter'). Prima SI and Allakhazam 2003 give level 60, Plant. Faction 98 Outland Residents matches the period 'lowers faction' list. No period description of the look exists, so model 946 (the only 1.64 treant) at size 50 is my choice. The loot hookup exists but its LootTemplate is empty, and the seed items lack their period charges. Region 181 has no navmesh, so Z comes from nearby rows.

## Skona Yecats

## Lough Gur Unseelie Envoy escort

### siabra escort (PLACE, 2 spawn(s), level 48-48)

Position: Next to the live 'unseelie envoy' row 96a684aa-74ce-44d7-9f7c-188451ca22e3 (334799,742184; Lough Gur south edge, on the convoy route). Both points were checked with K.ground (6838 and 6895, within 64). There is no CapnBry or Uthgard data for Lough Gur (zone 204). The Prima Atlas Lough Gur map (PDF p96) puts the 'SUPERNATURAL STALLION / UNSEELIE ENVOY / SIABRA ESCORT' labels together at about world 312.4-315.1k, 702.9-705.6k (fitted to 13 named NPCs, error about 1-4k). That spot is the Lough Gur end of the route, near the pooka spawn west of Innis. The escorts are a moving convoy, so these two rows are the convoy's starting slots, not a camp.

PARENT ENCOUNTER: the Unseelie Envoy convoy (Lough Gur / Bog of Cullen, region 200). The convoy is the envoy, 2 siabra escorts and 2 supernatural stallions walking a long route as one group. It is not a boss with adds called at % health. No period source mentions health-% adds, special attacks or despawns.

MEMBERS: 1 'unseelie envoy' (live row 96a684aa-74ce-44d7-9f7c-188451ca22e3, template 60167452, model 334 Elf Male 1; Prima lv55). 2 'supernatural stallion' (live rows d91bd52f-4837-4ae1-b1d4-17ed24eee52a and 470ade65-3d8c-4266-bd71-07e7d58623d6, template 60166634; Prima lv48; change model 2132 to 414 'Nightmare', the pooka look, because 2132 is not in the 1.64 client). 2 'siabra escort' (new, template 60165982). Today all of them are static DOL.GS.GameNPC rows with RoamingRange 200, and stallion 470ade65 stands 8k east of the others.

ROUTE (world coords, region 200):
(A) Home: the base of the Siabra Queen's tower in the Siabra city, Bog of Cullen. Queen Cliodna stands on the upper floor at 388237,752506,5722; the ground at the base is about 388637,752506,5040. Source: Verena, Freddyshouse, 2002-05-05: the group stands at the base of the Siabra queen tower.
(B) The land bridge near Siopa: 361569,745050 (ground 4959). Sources: the Zam bestiary loc '9313, 32346 Bog of Cullen' on all three mob pages (page updated 2008, loc not dated); boni_ofdavoid, Freddyshouse, 2003-02-04: they 'headed off to siabra city via the land bridge near Siopia'. The Siopa merchants are at about 361.3k,750.3k.
(C) Lough Gur, south-east, where the live envoy and stallions stand now: 334799,742184,6857 and 342198,742938,7255.
(D) The Lough Gur turnaround, near the pooka spawn west of Innis Carthaig: the Prima Atlas map label cluster at about 312400-315100, 702900-705600 (ground 4219-4624). Supporting posts: josua, 2002-10-09 ('Pookahs West of innis ... we have seen a convoy'); boni, 2003-02-04 ('from near the pook spawn at innis towards the bog').
HARD RULE: the route must NOT pass through Innis Carthaig (merchants and guards at 333.8-335.6k, 718.9-721.1k). Source: the 1.42 release notes (Warcry patchs/020115, about Jan 2002): 'A bug was found with the path of the Unseelie Envoy and his escort. His escorts and stallions should no longer ... cross through Innis to return home.' The straight line from D to B runs right through Innis, so the path must go D -> south around Innis (through C) -> B -> A. Build the in-between waypoints on the navmesh (K.ground); no period waypoints exist beyond A-D.

TIMING: one out-and-back trip: A -> B -> C -> D, short stop, D -> C -> B -> A, then rest at A. Source: Verena, 2002-05-05: 'Every morning they go out on a patrol all the way into Lough Gur and head back to the Siab town in the afternoon.'
